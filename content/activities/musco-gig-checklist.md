## 🎚️ What this is

The field checklist for a gig at **Musco Center for the Arts**. It's the same idea as the
**Salmon Gig Checklist** — every line is something you do or check, the "why" lives elsewhere —
but the room is different: you bring the gear over yourself, you work alongside the Musco crew,
and several cameras have to match each other.

## 🗝️ Key terms

**stage door** · **front of house (FOH)** · **concert lighting** · **brightest cue** · **board
feed** · **video switcher** · **headset (comms)** · **house default settings** · **call time**

## 👔 Dress code

- 🚩 **All black**, head to toe.
- 🚩 **Closed-toed shoes.**

This applies to every gig, Salmon included — you're visible from the house and backstage.

## 🧰 Equipment and materials

Pack this before you leave BH 208/209.

- [ ] The **black backpack with the red stripes** (the Standard Gear Kit), containing:
  - [ ] **Sony AX100** camera
  - [ ] **AX100 power cable** — don't count on the battery for a full concert
  - [ ] **Zoom H4n** recorder
- [ ] **SD cards: 128 GB, 32 GB, and 8 GB**, all clean
- [ ] **Fresh AA batteries for the H4n** — rechargeables are fine. The H4n takes two; bring
  **four**, in case some turn out to be dead.
- [ ] **Tripod** — and confirm the **plate on the AX100 matches this tripod** before you leave
- [ ] **The stand** — see **Technical verification**
- [ ] **SSDs** — two or three, so there are options
- [ ] **Headphones** with a **¼-inch adapter**

## 🚗 Pre-arrival

- 🚩 Check for unfinished business from the last gig — cards or SSDs that still need cleaning, or
  a data transfer that never got finished. Finish that first.
- 🚩 Dressed per the **Dress code**.
- 🚩 If you're going to be late, text your fellow engineer(s) before your call time.

**Q1 (short answer).** Call time: ______ · Concert: ______ · Arrival time: ______

✅ **Answer.** There's no fixed answer — write in tonight's call time, tonight's concert, and when
you actually got there. It's a log entry, not a test.

## 🚪 Arrival

- 🚩 Walk the gear over to Musco.
- 🚩 Go in through the **stage door** and **sign in**.
- 🚩 Make your way around to **front of house**.
- 🚩 Get a **headset** — on most gigs everyone on the crew has one so you can talk to each other.

## 🙋 First: the things that need other people

Do these before anything you can do on your own — other people's time is the bottleneck.

- 🚩 Ask the lighting crew for **concert lighting**, including the **brightest cue** of the night,
  so exposure gets set against the real light, not work lights.
- 🚩 Check the **board feed**.
- 🚩 Decide **who's on the video switcher**.

## 🎥 Cameras — exposure and color

- 🚩 Set exposure against the **brightest cue** — that's the one most likely to blow out.
- 🚩 **Every camera matches**: same exposure, same color. A cut between cameras shouldn't look
  like a cut between rooms.
- 🚩 Start from the **house default settings** — see **Technical verification**.

## 🎙️ Backup audio — Zoom H4n

- 🚩 Fresh AA batteries in.
- 🚩 Clean SD card in.
- 🚩 Headphones on (¼-inch adapter) — **check the backup recording** and confirm the signal is
  clean.
