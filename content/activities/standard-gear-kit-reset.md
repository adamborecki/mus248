> 🧪 **Status: In Development.** Started October 2026. The camera defaults, card sizes, and
> where the kit lives come from the Standard Gear Kit Checklist; the F8 power and data-safety
> steps come from the Salmon Gig Checklist. Steps marked **(to confirm)** haven't been confirmed
> yet — see **Technical verification**.

## 🎚️ What this is

The state the Standard Gear Kit goes back in at the end of every gig, so the next engineer starts
from a known setup instead of whatever the last person left. It covers any Standard Gear Kit gig,
including the [Memorial Hall Gig Checklist](../../memorial-gig-checklist/). The gig itself is the
[Standard Gear Kit Checklist](../../standard-gear-kit-checklist/); this is only the putting-back.

## ⚠️ Before you touch anything

- 🚫 **Never erase or format an SD card** until every file on it has passed the Triple Triple
  Check onto both drives. If you're not sure, leave the card as it is and ask.
- ☝️ Every recorder is **stopped** before anything gets unplugged.

## 🎙️ Audio

**Zoom F8**

- 🚩 **Phantom power off** on every input.
- 🚩 Power off — hold the power switch **~2 seconds**.
- 🚩 Unplug **AC power**.
- 🚩 Dual Channel Recording left as you found it (to confirm).

**Zoom H4n**

- 🚩 Power off.
- 🚩 Batteries out and charging (to confirm).

**Mics, stands, and cables**

- 🚩 Pencil condensers off the stereo bar and back in their cases (to confirm).
- 🚩 Boundary mics, if you used them, back in the kit.
- 🚩 XLR-to-¼-inch TRS adapter back in the kit.
- 🚩 Anything the house lent you, such as a board-feed cable, back to house staff.
- 🚩 Cables coiled, Velcro on.
- 🚩 Mic stand, stereo bar, and lighting stand collapsed.

## 🎥 Video — Sony AX100s

- 🚩 Both cameras back to the default exposure: **f/4.0 · 3 dB gain · 1/60 sec**.
- 🚩 White balance back to **Indoor** on both.
- 🚩 Power off; batteries charging, or AC unplugged.
- 🚩 Tripods collapsed; tripod plates left on the cameras (to confirm).

## 💾 Media

- 🚩 Every card accounted for: the F8's **32 GB**, the H4n's **8 GB**, and both AX100s' **128 GB**.
- 🚩 Cards cleared only after the Triple Triple Check — see the Salmon Gig Checklist's **Data
  transfer** section (to confirm for this kit).

## 🚚 Back to BH 208/209

- 🚩 Everything back in BH 208/209, where it came from.
- 🚩 Anything broken, missing, or low on batteries reported to the instructor.

## ✅ Definition of done

- 🚩 Every recorder stopped before anything was unplugged.
- 🚩 Phantom power off, and every device powered down.
- 🚩 Both AX100s back at f/4.0 · 3 dB · 1/60 · Indoor.
- 🚩 No card erased before its files passed the Triple Triple Check.
- 🚩 The house got its own gear back, and the kit is back in BH 208/209.
