 📁What Can You Do With a Mixer
(Key for Difficulty)
Based on ski slope difficulty levels
🟢 = Easy
🟦 = Intermediate
⬥ = Hard
⬥⬥ = Expert
⁉️= Unknown or variable difficulty depending on hardware


→ Essential skills / getting started
* If you’re getting started, do this order:
   * 1️⃣ Single mic PA
   * 2️⃣ Controlled feedback
   * 3️⃣ Stereo playback
   * 4️⃣ Stage monitors with main mix
   * 5️⃣ Multitrack PA (imaginary mics, real music)
* After you can do those, see how many other things you can accomplish!
* Mix & Match
   * Note that these tasks are not mutually exclusive.
      * E.g. to get “controlled feedback”, you need a mic and speaker set up, even though that isn’t explicitly stated in the instructions.
Inputs, Mains, & Feedback
* 🟢 Single mic PA
   *  → 1️⃣
   * Overview
      * Connect 1 mic and at least 1 speaker to a mixing board
   * Essential Pro Tips:
      * In live sound setup:
         * Always turn speakers ON last
      * In live sound teardown:
         * Always turn speakers OFF first
   * Prove it works
      * Take a video that demonstrates talking 
      * Upload that video to OneDrive and turn it in on Canvas when you submit your activity report
   * Details
      * 48V required for condensers
      * Know your cables types
* 🟦 Controlled Feedback
   * →  2️⃣
   * Overview:
      * Try to get feedback on the mic
   * Prove it works:
      * Record a video demonstrating controlled feedback
      * Find out which frequency is feeding back
   * Details:
      * You’ll pretty much always want your mains in front of the band (not behind) to reduce the likelihood of feedback.
* ⬥⬥ Ring out the room
   * Overview:
      * Using a mixer that has a digital EQ on the main fader (e.g. the Yamaha 01V or Behringer x32) to apply one or more notch filters
   * Prove it works:
      * Record a video that has controlled feedback that’s removed when the EQ is applied.
* 🟢 Stereo playback
   * → 3️⃣
   * Overview:
      * Connect a stereo playback device (e.g. laptop, 3.5mm phone connector)
   * Prove it works:
      * Record a video of playing a “Stereo test video” on youtube (or anything similar) coming across on the PA
      * It should clearly show the left channel vs. the right channel
* 🟢 Multitrack PA (real mics, imaginary music)
   * Overview:
      * Connect multiple real microphones to a mixer and test them out
      * Make sure at least 1 mic requires 48V phantom power
   * Prove it works:
      * Record a video demonstrating multiple mics connected to PA
* 🟢 Use a DI (Direct Input) box
   * Overview
      * Use a DI box to capture the signal from a guitar, bass, or guest 3.5mm aux cable
   * Prove it works
      * Record a video that shows the signal flow
   * Details
      * Active DIs require 48V, passive DIs do not
* 🟦 Multitrack PA (imaginary mics, real music)
   *  → 5️⃣
   * Overview:
      * Use a computer with a DAW and audio interface that has multitrack outputs
      * Get multitrack stems (from somewhere like Cambridge Mixing stems)
      * Assign 1 output for each track to a discrete output on the audio interface
      * Mix as if there was a live band
   * Prove it works
      * Record a video that demonstrates the “imaginary mics” working with your PA
Signal Flow
* 🟦 Stage Monitors with main mix (less common)
   * → 4️⃣
   * Overview
      * Connect a stage monitor and send the main mix to it
* ⬥ Stage Monitors with custom mixes (more common!)
   * Overview
      * Use your mixing board to send custom mixes (different than main mix) to the monitors
* 🟢 Board feed recording
   * Overview
      * Record audio from the board onto a separate 2 track recorder
* ⁉️ Multitrack recording
   * Overview
      * Get a recording that has every channel.
   * Details
      * Depending on the board, this could be easy or difficult
   * Prove it works
      * Record a video demonstrating it and record the multitrack stems
Audio Effects
* 🟢 FX: EQ
   * Overview
      * Try using a low cut on everything except bass and line level (guest 3.5mm) inputs
      * Try using EQ on each channel
   * Prove it works
      * Record a video demonstrating the color change by using an EQ
* ⁉️ FX: Compressor
   * Overview
      * Try using a compressor on the channel
   * Prove it works
      * Do a demo of something that’s very dynamic (ie.., you whispering “quiet” then screaming “LOUD”) to show that the compressor works.
      * Record that dynamic thing (whisper “quiet” then scream “loud) first without the compressor, then with it
* ⬥ FX: Reverb
   * Overview
      * Set up an aux bus with a reverb send
   * Prove it works
      * Record a video demonstrating something without reverb then with reverb
