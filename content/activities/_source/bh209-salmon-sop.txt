KNOWN STALE. The instructor confirmed in September 2026 that parts of this have
already changed. Do not follow the audio steps without checking:
  - the multitrack front end is now a ZOOM F8, not the MOTU 8PreX described here,
    so the preamp positions, I/O settings and buffer notes are all suspect
  - the stereo handheld is probably a ZOOM H6, not the H4n named here (unconfirmed)
  - "the computer in the room" is ambiguous: BH 208/209 holds several Macs
  - the AX100 lives in BH 208/209 storage and is carried down to the hall per gig
  - gain and f-stop are preset because the hall's lighting is constant; a rare
    low-light piece is a real exception, and the engineer is expected to deviate
    knowingly and put it back

================================================================================
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (S.O.P.)
================================================================================

Pre-show:
[] Get Checklist
  Printed checklists should be available in BH 208.

Prepare audio:
Power: [] Battery backup ON [] "Salmon Audio Rec" Mac Mini ON [] 8PreX ON (down)
  [] Genelec 8030's [] H4n new batteries [] H4n AC

  Battery Backup
  Battery backup: holding power button for two seconds will turn battery backup on
  or off; if lightbulb indicator is lit, battery backup is on.

  Genelec 8030's
  The switch for the speakers power is on the rack, above the mic splitter.

  iMac
  Power button is located on the back left side; the computer may automatically turn
  on when the backup battery is turned on.

  H4n New Batteries & H4n AC
  H4n requires two AA batteries inserted into the back of the device; batteries are
  near the backup battery. AC power is plugged into the center of the bottom of the
  H4n recorder. The power switch is on the bottom of the left side.

Data: [] 8GB SD card for H4n [] 32GB SD card for iMac
  Smaller SD cards are found in a clear plastic case; use 8GB for H4n and 32GB for
  iMac. SD cards are stored on the left side (BH 209).

Setup H4n: [] Mic Input level of ext 1-2: 28 [] XLRs connected (Green: Left, Red: Right)
Listen to H4n: [] Record Enable (to get signal) [] Clean sound [] Proper Stereo
  Use headphones to check the audio signals of the H4n.

Logic Project: [] Opened from Template [] Named "YYMMDD [abbr_concert_name]"
  [] Saved as a Folder [] to the Desktop
  To Open a New Project in Logic: File > New from template > My templates >
    "Salmon Concert Template"
  To Save the Project: File > Save As > filename: [YYMMDD Concert Name]
    (ex. 220117 TCO) > location: either desktop or directly onto 32GB SD card

Logic Preferences: [] I/O Focusrite Thunderbolt [] 1024 Buffer Size
  To Open Preferences: Logic Pro > Preferences > Audio
  Check that input and output both list "Focusrite Thunderbolt".
  Check that the I/O Buffer Size is 1024.

8PreX: [] Preamps (1&2 "2:00", 3&4 "11:00", 5&6 "3:00", 7&8 "10:00")
  Check the preamps on the Focusrite are pointing to the directions listed.

Listen to Logic: [] Rec Enable on all channels [] Clean Signal on Ch. 1_2 (& 3_4)
  [] Clean Signal on Ch. 5-6
  Use the Speakers to check the logic project. If the speakers are off, turn them on
  with the power conditioner at the top of the rack.
  If there is no audio in Salmon, use the talkback to check signal.
  H4n needs XLR cables in L and R.
  Logic project should have channels armed and input monitoring on.
  Channels 7&8 are for the "board feed" (i.e. signal from the "FOH" board, i.e. the
  Mackie iPad mixer in the hall).

Prepare video:
Power: [] ATEM Master [] AC for AX100(s) [] Restart and Login Dell Touchscreen
  Turn on ATEM Master switch to provide power for ATEM Extreme, ATEM HyperDeck, and
  monitors.
  AX100 cameras use AC power plugged into the front of the camera; also have a fully
  charged battery for backup power.
  Restart the Dell touchscreen computer and log in using the music recordings account
  information (sheet of paper with password located in front of the computer).

Data: [] SATA SSD for HyperDeck [] 128GB SD card(s) for backup wide
  Plug in a clean SATA SSD to the HyperDeck for 4k backup recording.
  Insert 128 GB SD card to AX100 backup wide; large SD cards are found in plastic
  folders.

Primary Center wide: [] Zoom on Dell with 'Rovocontrol' [] PTZ with Joystick
  [] shot "as close as possible" BUT wide enough to have enough space on edges while
  standing to bow
  Use 'Rovocontrol' app to adjust the zoom of the camera; you can view your changes
  on the monitors. GAIN AND F-STOP DO NOT CHANGE, only change zoom control at the
  bottom.
  Adjust framing (pan/tilt) with joystick.

Backup center wide: [] WB: 3800K A5 G2 [] Image Quality/Size: XAVCS 4K
  [] Not into Extreme ISO [] shot "as close as possible" BUT wide enough to have
  enough space on edges while standing to bow
  AX100 Exposure Settings: F4, 3dB, 1/60
  AX100 White Balance: 3800K, A5 G2, Soft High Key
  AX100 Image Size: Menu > Image Quality/Size > XAVC S 4K
  Ensure that the HDMI is not plugged into the ATEM Extreme.

Prepare multicam video: (Recording Tier 4)
(if multicam:) [] Battery swap for GH5s
  Put fresh batteries in GH5s and charge other batteries — "CHARGE" indicator light
  turns off when battery is fully charged.
(if multicam:) [] Frame all cameras and check exposure settings
  GH5 Exposure Settings: F4, 400ISO, 1/30
  GH5 White Balance: 3700K, B4 G/M0
  GH5 Picture Effect: Menu > Photo Style > Picture Effect: CineD: -3, -2, 0, -4, -3
  GH5 Image Size: Menu > Record format > 4K, 100M, 30fps
  See also: "Multicam Exposure / Color / Formats Setting" (Google Doc)
(if multicam:) [] 128 GB SD cards for all cameras [] USB-C SSD for ATEM Extreme
  More clean 128 GB cards can be found in the same folders.
  Plug in USB-C SSD for the ATEM Extreme; cable is located in the USB A slot.

Lobby & Hall:
Program: [] Get program [] Ask about changes [] Ask about encore
  There are often physical printed programs and a QR code you can scan to open the
  program.
  Ask (the recital manager? the performer?) if there are any changes to the program.
  Sometimes there are changes: a piece being omitted, or the order rearranged.
  Sometimes there's an encore: an additional piece that isn't listed on the program.
  If there are any files, be sure to get (1) title and (2) composer (and arranger,
  if applicable).

Voice of God: [] Audio check [] Reset as "1 push" for recital manager
  Turn on the audio rack; make sure both the rack and the Mackie mixer are on. The
  voice of god is in the "Numark" CD Player — press play to check audio levels. Use
  the track knob to go to the beginning of Track 1 (the pre-show announcement) and
  leave it set this way for the recital manager to play.

Lobby Feed After doors Open: [] Hallway Audio [] Hallway TV camera
  Once the doors open and the audience starts to enter, tap the CRESTRON controller
  to view the touchscreen. Select "Salmon" for Hallway Audio and "Camera" for Hallway
  TV. Sometimes the recital managers will come up to BH 209 and ask for these.
  The lobby can be viewed using MON 2.

Show:
Start recording 3 minutes early:
  ATEM HyperDeck: INSTRUCTIONS COMING
  Backup wide: press red record button to the left of the viewfinder on the AX100
  Logic: make sure all tracks are armed (the R is red) and input monitoring is on
    (the I is yellow), then press record
  H4n: press the record button and make sure the numbers begin moving
  ATEM Extreme (multicam): press REC button in the top right corner and check the
    monitor to make sure it is recording
  GH5 (multicam): press the record button on the right (near the front) of the camera

Monitor recording statuses:
  Ensure all systems are still recording often, and that there is enough space
  remaining on all storage devices.
  (multicam) Occasionally adjust multicam view (or follow soloists).
  (multicam) Use the ATEM Extreme switcher for program feed, variety of angles in
  the main feed.

Live stream monitoring: [] Watch & listen on a separate machine.

Intermission:
[] Pause all recordings — pause all recording devices as close together as possible.
   If needed, replace SSD or SD cards if there is not enough space for the second half.
[] Start all recordings again 3 minutes early.

Post-Show:
Power Down
[] Check all gear that you turned on, and turn it back off
[] Don't turn off things that aren't supposed to be turned off

Data Transfer & Transcoding in BOTH Post and Backup drives:
[] Create concert folder using YYMMDD filename
   File > New Folder > Filename: YYMMDD [Concert Name] (ex. 220117 TCO)
   In the backup drives add BAK after the concert name (ex. 220117 TCO BAK).
   Create e/edited and r/raw folders inside the concert folder.
[] Copy files from SD cards into new folders titled "from [SD CARD NAME]"
   In the r/raw folder create a folder titled "from [SD CARD NAME]"
   (ex. from ChapMus128G) for each SD or SSD used in the recording.

Clean-up:
[] Turn off Lobby Feed — set CRESTRON controller: select "Signage" for Hallway TV,
   select "OFF" for Hallway Audio
[] Remove and put away any gear from Salmon — if the gig used any gear (mic stands,
   mics, cables, monitors, etc.) clean it up and return it to where it came from
   (BH 208/209, instrument checkout room, etc.)
[] Turn off audio and video equipment — ATEM Master power, backup battery, individual
   cameras, etc.
[] Leave BH 208/209 as tidy as possible and turn off lights

Day after:
[] Complete data transfer (if not completed day of). SD cards may have been moved to
   a used SD card sleeve.
[] Delete files from SD cards using "Triplets Triple Triple Checked" procedure.
   Clean SD cards so the next gig will have enough.
Handbrake ProRes 4K to h.265: as in the checklist above.

Weeks after:
[] Keep Microsoft Planner task updated — use tasks.office.com to move the gig from
   one bucket to another to show progress in post production.

Within 1 week:
  Uploaded to Google Drive: [] Full concert single cam mixed edited video
    [] Audio Only file
  Within one week of the concert, upload a single cam mixed edited video of the gig.
  This is uploaded into the Chapman Music Recording Archives on Google Drive.
  Choose the correct time period, and create a folder with the conventional filename
  (YYMMDD [Concert Name]). Upload the full concert video and audio only files.
  [] Email music recordings to confirm completion

Within 3 weeks:
  Uploaded to Google Drive: [] Individual mixed edited video files [] Audio Only files
  This can be done using transplitting — see procedure 4a.
  Best Practice for naming: ## [Piece Name] [Take # (if applicable)]
    (ex. 01 Moonlight Sonata) or (ex. 03 Rhapsody in Blue Take 2)
  In the correct folder create subfolders for Individual Video and Individual Audio
  Only, and upload corresponding files.
  [] Email music recordings to confirm completion
  [] Upload all files to Post drive — if edited from a home computer, upload all post
     files and materials into the e/edited folder of the post drive. If edited in
     BH 208/209, render to the e/edit folder.
  [] Upload all files to Panopto. You may need to manually change the order.
