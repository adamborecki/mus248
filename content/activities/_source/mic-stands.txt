--- Slide 5  [5 images]
    Warm-Up 1: Upward Salute & Child's Pose
    Extend mic stands as much as possible (a.k.a. "Upward Salute")
    Collapse mic stands as much as possible (a.k.a. "Child's pose")
    Repeat 2-3x total

--- Slide 10  [2 images]
    Seriously, be careful!
    When fully extended, the center of weight changes: if a mic is on the boom arm and the leg is in the wrong position, the entire stand could fall over.
    In fact, this is why some stands have "counterweights".
    counterweight
