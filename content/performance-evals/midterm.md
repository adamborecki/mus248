Three activities, done one at a time, by yourself, with a notecard. You can start any time from
Week 7; they run until about Week 9 or 10.

## 🧭 How it works

1. Bring your notecard to class: **one per activity**, handwritten, front and back OK.
2. Lock up your phone and laptop.
3. You get the printed sheet: what each activity asks and what "done" means, but no step-by-step
   instructions.
4. Start the timer.
5. Do the activity by yourself, using your notecard.
6. Get the instructor to check your work when you're done.

> The Salmon Gig Checklist is not part of the midterm.
