Like the midterm: you show specific skills, one station at a time, by yourself. The goal is that
everyone who passes MUS 248 can work the normal gig types, not that you specialize early.

## 🧭 How it works

1. Every station is required.
2. Same procedure as the midterm: one handwritten notecard per station (front and back OK), phone
   and laptop locked up, the printed sheet, a timer, and you do it by yourself.
3. A checker watches and checks your work.
4. Retakes are allowed if there's time, but not if you put a station off until the end.
5. Already ran the Salmon Gig Checklist for real? It doesn't count here: the Salmon station is
   tested on its own.
6. Each station ends the way a gig does: everything put back the way you found it. (Planned; to
   be confirmed.)
