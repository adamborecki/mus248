Training: Post Mini

Bite-sized post-production jobs, each with real raw files and video tutorials. Do them in order: each phase assumes the ones before it.

Phase 1✂️ Sync, Trim & ExportOne video file, one audio file: sync by hand, trim, fade, limiter, export H.264 and WAV. Phase 2🎥 Multicam “Search” DemoTwo cameras, no separate audio: multicam clip, best angle, exposure and framing fixes. Phase 3🎼 Salmon RecitalLogic mix, 4K complete concert file, then one file per piece and per movement (01, 02a…). Phase 4🎙️ Salmon Recording SessionTakes instead of a concert: one file per take, take notes, backup audio, Normalize off.
Phase 5🚧 Two-half concert Not readyTwo halves of video and too-quiet pre-mixed audio, with talking and an encore. Coming later.
