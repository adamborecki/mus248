            ❓ How Sushi works
            1. Look. Tap a plate to read what the job is, where, how long, and what to check first. Looking never takes it.
            2. Take the Plate. It moves to My Table. Everyone else just sees “Taken”, never your name.
            3. Stuck? Call the Chef tells Adam. The plate stays yours.
            4. Finished Eating. Add a note if it asks for one. Some plates clear right away; others wait for Adam to check.
            5. Can't do it after all? Put It Back and someone else can take it.
            Small · 5–10 min Medium · 10–25 min Large · 25–60 min Boat · 60+ min project

            Plate size is time, not points: there are no scores here. Sushi keeps a record of work done; Canvas is still where grades live, and the university payroll system is still where hours get paid. Your name goes only to Adam's private sheet. It's typed, not a login, so it isn't proof of anything on its own.
